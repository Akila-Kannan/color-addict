THE RULES

Easy to learn.
Hard to think.

01

A word appears in a different-colored ink. Watch the prompt: WORD means read the word. INK means name its color.

02

Tap the matching color as quickly as you can. First correct answer gets 2 points; other correct answers get 1 point.

03

Wrong answers lock you out for that card. When a correct answer lands, the next card comes up shortly after.

04

After the host’s chosen number of cards, the highest score wins. A tie goes to one sudden-death card.

Every color is labeled, so everyone can play.
